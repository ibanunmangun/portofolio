Method and limits

Administrative footprint, not inundation extent

The map extracts Aceh Tamiang subdistrict geometry from the HDX COD-AB Indonesia administrative-boundary release and highlights the five subdistricts named in a 16 November 2026 report. The map does not infer a physical flood perimeter from those boundaries.

Muted terrain relief is a local derivative of Copernicus WorldDEM-30. It provides general terrain context only. Optional NASA GIBS imagery is fixed to 16 November 2026 and is reference imagery, not event-date flood evidence.

Satellite flood mapping needs event-specific validation and can miss or misclassify water in built-up or densely vegetated areas. No reproducible event-specific satellite extent was included in this case study.
