Method and boundary

From signal to review unit

NASA FIRMS VIIRS NOAA-20 NRT thermal detections were retrieved as a fixed September 2026 snapshot, then clipped to Central Kalimantan using HDX administrative boundaries. The source records represent thermal signals, not confirmed fire incidents.

For this demonstrator, nearby signals are grouped into transparent review units using an approximately 10 km grid cell and a three-day time bucket. Clusters provide a place to start contextual review. They do not establish cause, responsibility, burned area, or response priority.
